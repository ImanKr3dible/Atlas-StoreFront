ATLAS Refund & Cancellation Policy

Final refund eligibility and timing will be confirmed before live checkout is enabled.

Canceling a subscription

Recurring plans can be canceled through the billing portal. Unless applicable law requires otherwise, cancellation is intended to stop the next renewal while access continues through the already-paid billing period.

Refund requests

Approved refunds can be reconciled with the ATLAS entitlement system so refunded licenses can be revoked consistently. The final refund window and eligibility rules will be displayed before live purchase.

Billing errors

Customers should contact support promptly about duplicate, unauthorized, or incorrect charges so the issue can be investigated.

Mandatory rights

This policy does not limit consumer rights that cannot legally be waived.
